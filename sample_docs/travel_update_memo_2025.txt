MEMORANDUM
TO: All Staff, Acme Global Enterprises
FROM: Executive Operations & Finance Committee
DATE: January 15, 2025
SUBJECT: Policy Update - Travel & Expense Revisions for Fiscal Year 2025

Please be advised that the Executive Committee has ratified immediate amendments to the Corporate Travel Policy:

1. Revised Daily Meal Allowance:
Effective January 1, 2025, the maximum daily meal allowance for domestic business travel has been increased to  per day to account for cost-of-living inflation across key hubs. All other receipt requirements remain in force.

2. Expedited Booking Timelines:
To support agile client engagements, the travel request notice period is reduced: domestic travel bookings now require only 3 business days advance notice with department head sign-off, replacing the previous 14-day rule.

3. Ride-Share and Micro-mobility:
Employees may expense authorized ride-share (Uber/Lyft) and public transit expenses up to  daily for intra-city commute during business visits.

Please direct any questions to travel-desk@acmeglobal.com.
